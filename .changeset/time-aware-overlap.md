Double-bookings in the resource view are now time-aware. Two timed events on the same day only count (`hasOverlap`, `overlapDays`, and `data-overlap`) when their times overlap, so a morning job and an afternoon job no longer show as double-booked. All-day events still overlap anything on their days. Resource cells have a new `hasOverlap` field.
